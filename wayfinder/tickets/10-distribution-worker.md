## Goal

Implement the autonomous distribution loop under the policy in ticket 03.

## Required behavior

- Inspect current business state, evidence, budget, and prior results.
- Form and store a distribution hypothesis.
- Decide what human expertise or production work is needed.
- Create a task-specific Terac brief and acceptance rubric.
- Track feasibility, spend, submissions, and accepted work.
- Select the target, channel, campaign, and execution tools within policy.
- Commission follow-on human work when its plan requires it.
- Execute approved action classes without requesting founder approval for each routine step.
- Handle consent, privacy, outreach, and opt-out rules.
- Measure qualified prospects, case starts, paid cases, and customer-acquisition cost.
- Continue, revise, stop, or change strategy based on evidence and stop-loss rules.

Do not hard-code one audience, channel, campaign, or Terac job. Do hard-code policy enforcement, budget enforcement, evidence capture, idempotency, and stop conditions.
