## Manager objective

Acquire eligible Recoup customers at a sustainable customer-acquisition cost. The manager can change its audience, channel, message, and work plan when evidence supports the change.

## Manager loop

1. Inspect product truth, prior campaign evidence, budget, season, and current conversion data.
2. Identify the largest distribution uncertainty.
3. Decide whether it needs research, strategy, creative production, outreach labor, or another human contribution.
4. Request Terac feasibility for the required expertise without assuming that the audience is available.
5. Create and launch the selected Terac opportunity within policy.
6. Review submissions against a task-specific rubric, reject weak work, and retry or change the brief when needed.
7. Select an audience, channel, campaign, and execution tools from the available evidence.
8. Commission additional human work when the selected campaign needs content, creative assets, prospect research, or permitted outreach support.
9. Execute within the approved policy and budget.
10. Measure results, update its beliefs, stop weak campaigns, and allocate the next cycle.

## Fixed policy, not fixed strategy

Authority model approved 2026-08-15: **policy and budget autonomy**. Routine actions inside the policy do not require founder approval.

First-cycle Terac budget approved 2026-08-15: **the full available $125 Terac credit balance, with zero personal cash charge**. The manager uses the founder's authenticated Terac MCP or API connection, requests feasibility, reserves the quoted cost before launch, and stops before committed Terac credit use would exceed the available $125 balance. Credentials never enter the repository or chat.

First-cycle geographic scope approved 2026-08-15: **United States only**. The manager cannot advertise support for lease systems outside the approved product market.

First-cycle paid-media policy corrected 2026-08-15: **no cash budget for paid ads**. The earlier shared-budget interpretation is superseded. The manager can use organic publishing and direct outreach through connected accounts.

Account authority approved 2026-08-15: **connected accounts only**. The manager cannot create a new platform identity, accept new platform terms, or add a payment method. After the founder connects an account, the manager can perform routine policy-approved actions through it.

The founder defines:

- Product claims and prohibited claims
- Eligible customer and jurisdiction boundaries imposed by the product
- Total and per-action budgets
- Allowed and prohibited action classes
- Consent, privacy, outreach, and opt-out rules
- Public publishing and paid-media approval thresholds
- Success metrics and stop-loss rules

The manager decides:

- Target segment within the eligible market
- Channel mix
- Campaign concept and message
- Which Terac experts or workers to hire
- Which submissions to accept
- Which campaign tools to use
- When to continue, revise, stop, or switch strategy

## Evidence requirement

Every cycle records the manager's hypothesis, evidence, Terac brief, cost, submissions, acceptance decisions, campaign actions, results, and next decision. Autonomy is demonstrated by adaptation, not by following a hard-coded campaign.

## Resolution

The manager's authority model, market, account boundary, Terac credit budget, paid-media limit, fixed policies, autonomous decisions, and evidence requirements are approved. Implementation can proceed.
