## Goal

Use real Terac submissions to improve a distribution artifact or decision selected by the manager.

## Required measurement

- Store the manager's hypothesis and the pre-Terac artifact or metric.
- Run the manager-selected Terac job within policy.
- Apply only accepted submissions.
- Measure the same artifact or business metric after accepted work is applied.
- Store the submissions, acceptance decisions, changes, metric, and cost.
- Continue measuring the real campaign result after launch.

The metric depends on the job. It can measure strategy quality, creative quality, qualified output, or a real campaign result. The manager must define it before launching the opportunity and explain why it matters to customer acquisition.
