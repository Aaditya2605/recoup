## Goal

Run one trace from a distribution trigger to a terminal case outcome without the founder performing routine business work.

## Evidence bundle

- Agent decisions and state transitions
- Manager hypothesis and target or channel decision
- Terac job selected by the manager and accepted output
- Real distribution action and resulting prospect state
- Customer offer and payment evidence, if payment is part of the approved trace
- Product intake and audit evidence
- Pioneer extraction and verification records
- Deterministic calculation records
- Customer approvals
- External communications and replies
- Terminal outcome

Synthetic, staged, and real elements must have separate labels. Terac is required only in the real role selected by the manager. The proof fails if a required external action exists only in the UI.
