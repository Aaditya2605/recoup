## Product surfaces

- Offer and eligibility
- Case intake and document upload
- Evidence and deadline status
- Verified findings with source and calculation
- Requests for missing evidence
- Communication draft
- Customer authorization and signature
- Case activity and terminal outcome

## Required states

Loading, empty, invalid document, extraction failure, ambiguous evidence, audit in progress, verified result, waiting for consent, external action pending, completed, and professional escalation.

The product must not show a payment, message, human review, or case outcome as completed until stored external evidence confirms it.
