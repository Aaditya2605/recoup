## Confirmed from `idea.md`

### Product

- Customer: commercial triple-net retail tenants, from one location to multi-location operators.
- Input: lease and amendments, projected CAM statement when separate, annual reconciliation, and payment ledger or total when needed.
- Service: determine the deadline, audit the reconciliation, obtain missing evidence, prepare and send approved notices, manage replies, and continue to a defined outcome.
- Result: corrected unpaid bill, rent credit, refund, confirmed charge, rejected dispute, or professional escalation.

### Distribution

- Timing: reconciliation season, mostly the first half of the year.
- Candidate channels observed in the thesis include search content, cold email, and professional referrals. They are options, not fixed implementation.
- The manager agent selects the audience, channel, message, human workers, and campaign based on evidence and results.
- The computed objection deadline is an available conversion fact, not a mandatory campaign device.
- Constraint: customer-acquisition cost is the main economic risk.

### Fulfillment

- The company agent reads documents, performs the audit, requests records, drafts notices, sends factual follow-ups, maintains the case, and reports the result.
- The customer supplies documents, signs formal notices, approves escalation, and forwards postal or telephone replies.
- A partner CPA performs and signs an audit when the lease requires an independent CPA.
- A qualified professional handles material legal or accounting interpretation.

## Resolution

Approved 2026-08-15: the distribution manager operates under policy and budget. It can hire Terac workers, send compliant outreach, publish content, and run campaigns without per-action founder approval while it remains within those controls. Ticket 03 defines the numeric limits and detailed policy.
