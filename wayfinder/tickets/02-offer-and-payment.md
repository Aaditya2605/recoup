## Confirmed from `idea.md`

The customer pays for one managed CAM reconciliation case. Recoup audits the documents and operates the case toward a correction, credit, refund, confirmed charge, or professional escalation.

- Version 1 serves commercial triple-net retail leases only.
- Both single-location and multi-location operators can buy.
- Required inputs are the lease and amendments, reconciliation, separate projected statement when applicable, and a ledger or payment total when needed.
- A confirmed correct charge is still a delivered assurance result.
- Version 1 uses a flat per-case fee. The working hypothesis is $200-$500.
- Recoup does not use a contingency fee.

Pricing model confirmed 2026-08-15: **flat per-case fee for version 1**. Annual monitoring remains a later option for multi-location and portfolio customers after real usage data exists.

Payment point confirmed 2026-08-15: **after a free eligibility check**. The check confirms version-1 scope, required documents, deadline viability, and obvious professional-escalation conflicts before checkout.

Launch price confirmed 2026-08-15: **$499 per eligible managed case**. This price excludes outside CPA, legal, certified-mail, and other disclosed third-party costs unless the offer explicitly includes them. The price is a market test, not a permanent price.

Refund rule confirmed 2026-08-15: **full refund** when a paid case is later found to be outside version-1 scope or cannot be processed from the supplied documents. A completed audit is not refundable only because it confirms that the landlord's charge is correct.

## Resolution

The version-1 offer, payment point, launch price, exclusions, and refund rule are approved. Stripe can be placed after the free eligibility check.
