## Confirmed from `idea.md`

- Arithmetic, share percentage, cap, and category errors can be verified from the lease and statement when those documents contain the required facts.
- Invoice-level and ledger-level claims require supporting records.
- A one-page statement without records limits verification to arithmetic, share, caps, and category names.
- The customer authorizes records requests and communication, signs formal notices, and approves each escalation.
- The lease determines the notice method. Certified mail is required when the lease requires physical delivery.
- The default payment guidance is pay under protest, then dispute, when the lease requires payment during the dispute.
- An agent cannot waive rights, accept a compromise, sign an agreement, send an unsigned formal notice, threaten legal action, or provide legal advice.
- A partner CPA is required when the audit clause requires an independent CPA.
- A qualified professional handles material legal or accounting interpretation.

## Genuine remaining question

Determine the jurisdiction-specific professional and representation rules before live customer cases.
