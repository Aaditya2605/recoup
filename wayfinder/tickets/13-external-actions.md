## Goal

Connect only the external tools selected by the approved company contracts.

## Possible categories

- Payment
- Distribution communication
- Customer communication
- Lease-compliant notice delivery
- Inbound reply capture
- Human work through Terac
- Model inference through Pioneer
- Hosting and durable workflow execution

## Tool acceptance test

For each selected tool, record the business state it changes, live call evidence, failure behavior, idempotency key, and reconciliation method. A configured key or imported SDK is not an integration proof.

## Deferred

Perflo is not part of version 1 or the hackathon build. Do not onboard it, fund an agent account, or claim its sponsor track. Reconsider it only when Recoup has company-funded working capital and a real autonomous purchase dependency.

## Excluded

Composio is not part of the version-1 architecture. Do not add it as an integration layer. Use the direct API, SDK, MCP, or OAuth connection approved for each required service.
