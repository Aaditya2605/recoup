## Mission

Build Recoup as an agent-run company with three connected parts:

1. Product: audit a commercial tenant's annual CAM reconciliation and operate the correction case.
2. Distribution: an autonomous manager discovers how to acquire customers, commissions human work through Terac when useful, executes through connected accounts, measures results, and changes strategy.
3. Fulfillment: agents read the documents, verify findings, request records, prepare approved notices, manage replies, and continue to a defined case outcome.

The founder sets policy and builds the system. The founder is not the routine marketer or case operator.

## Approved business decisions

### Product and fulfillment

- Version 1 serves commercial triple-net retail leases. The first distribution cycle targets the United States. Live service still requires jurisdiction-specific professional rules.
- The product accepts single-location and multi-location customers.
- Required case inputs and the customer workflow are defined in `idea.md`.
- The LLM interprets documents, extracts structured terms with source evidence, verifies findings, and drafts communications.
- A small deterministic calculator executes date and money formulas selected from the extracted rules.
- No finding enters a formal communication without source evidence and an independent verification pass.
- The customer signs formal notices, approves escalation, controls compromise, and retains legal rights.
- A partner CPA is required when the lease requires an independent CPA.
- A qualified professional handles material legal or accounting interpretation.
- Terminal outcomes are corrected unpaid bill, rent credit, refund, confirmed charge, rejected dispute, or professional review.

### Offer

- The customer receives a free eligibility check.
- An eligible managed case costs $499.
- The fee is flat per case. It is not contingent on recovery.
- Annual monitoring is deferred until real portfolio usage exists.
- Outside CPA, legal, certified-mail, and other disclosed third-party costs are excluded unless the offer explicitly includes them.
- Give a full refund when a paid case is later found outside version-1 scope or cannot be processed from the supplied documents.
- Do not refund a completed case only because the original landlord charge is confirmed correct.
- Stripe belongs after the free eligibility check.

### Distribution manager

- The manager has policy-and-budget autonomy. It does not request founder approval for each routine action.
- The manager chooses its target segment, channel, campaign, message, Terac jobs, workers, tools, and strategy from evidence.
- Do not hard-code one audience, channel, campaign, or Terac job.
- Geographic scope is the United States.
- The manager can use connected accounts only. It cannot create new accounts, accept new platform terms, or add a payment method.
- There is no paid-media cash budget in the first cycle.
- Organic publishing and direct outreach through connected accounts are allowed within policy.
- The manager can spend the full available $125 Terac credit balance.
- Terac spending must use the founder's authenticated account, request feasibility first, reserve the quoted credit cost, and stop before any personal cash charge.
- Every cycle records its hypothesis, evidence, human brief, cost, submissions, acceptance decisions, external actions, results, and next decision.

### Explicit exclusions

- Perflo is not part of version 1. Do not onboard or fund it and do not claim its sponsor track.
- Composio is not part of the architecture. Do not add it because it happens to be installed.
- Terac is not required in fulfillment. It must perform real, measurable work somewhere in the company. The distribution manager selects that work.
- Do not present synthetic cases, fake customers, fake payments, fake replies, or UI-only state as external proof.
